Hi! I am Cris, a neuroscience researcher and aspiring psychologist.

I completed a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering and post-doctoral fellowship in neuroscience, both at Johns Hopkins University (Baltimore, USA). Previously, I received a Masters in Biomedical Engineering (1st Hons) from Imperial College London (London, UK). I am currently completing additional coursework in psychology.

In my research, I worked with Dr. Amy Bastian to study how the perceptual experience of one’s own body shapes the way we move and learn in different environments. As I transition from neuroscience towards psychology, I hope to repurpose my expertise in self-perception and expand towards more complex cognitive and social processes. How does our perception of self and others shape our identity and social interactions? And how do these factors mediate well-being?
