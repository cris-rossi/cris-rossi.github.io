Hi! I am Cris, a psychology & neuroscience researcher interested in perception, social interaction & neurodiversity.

I am a postdoctoral fellow in Psychology at Karolinska Institute working with Dr. Arvid Guterstam to investigate the mechanistic basis and real-world implications of neurotype composition in dyadic social interactions (Stockholm, Sweden).

Previously, I completed a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering and post-doctoral fellowship in neuroscience, both at Johns Hopkins University (Baltimore, USA). I worked with Dr. Amy Bastian to study how the perceptual experience of one’s own body shapes the way we move and learn in different environments. I also completed a Masters in Biomedical Engineering (1st Hons) from Imperial College London (London, UK) and additional training in psychology.

Transitioning from neuroscience to psychology research, my expertise in self-perception grounds my investigation of more complex cognitive and social processes. How does our perception of self and others shape our identity and social interactions? And how do these factors mediate social functioning & well-being?
